ACK Guard

A deterministic payment policy and a signed audit trail for AI agents, built on Catena Labs' open-source Agent Commerce Kit.

Checking the audit log in your browser

This is a static replay. The three steps were recorded when this page was built, in the project's sandbox, with a deterministic scripted agent (not an LLM). The page makes no API calls. It re-checks the audit log's hash chain and signatures here, in your browser.

See it work in three steps

Sandbox only: fictional merchants, an in-memory ledger, no real money.

  1. A legitimate purchase

    The owner asks for tomorrow's Boston forecast. SkyCast Weather answers with a signed $0.25 ACK-Pay request.

  2. An injected invoice

    An email in the agent's inbox carries a $3.80 "overdue balance" payment request, signed by an attacker posing as SkyCast billing.

  3. Someone rewrites the record

    A copy of the signed log is edited so the blocked payment reads "allow", then verified in your browser.

Try your own edit

Pick an entry of the signed log and change it. The copy is re-verified in your browser; the original stays untouched.
